There is a small gland sitting at the exact center of your head. Ancient Hindus called it the third eye. Taoists called it the upper dantian. Descartes called it the seat of the soul. And for reasons this book spends 248 pages examining, almost nobody talks about it anymore. The Mercy Seat is a plain-spoken investigation into what the old texts said about that chamber, why the references to it kept getting trimmed out, and what has been quietly done to it in the last seventy years.
